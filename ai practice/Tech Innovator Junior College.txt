1 The TIJC IT Department operates an equipment room and wishes to implement a web-based IT equipment loan system to better manage inventory and track student loans.

Students can use the system to browse available equipment categories, request items for their projects, and track their loan history.

The web-based equipment loan system must be able to:

Store student details.

Store information related to equipment categories and specific devices.

Keep track of the equipment borrowed by students.

There are a total of four tables, and the table descriptions are as follows:

student (studentID, name, phoneNo)

category (categoryID, categoryName)

equipment (equipID, categoryID*, equipName, brand, availableQty)

loanRecord (studentID*, equipID*, loanDate, returnDate, quantity)

The primary key is indicated by underlining one or more attributes.
The attribute indicated with * denoted a foreign key.

It is given that:

Students are allowed to borrow different equipment in a single transaction. For example, when a student borrows three different devices on the same day, three separate records with the same value for studentID and loanDate will be inserted into the table loanRecord.

The primary keys categoryID and equipID of the tables category and equipment are auto-incremented integers.

loanDate and returnDate in table loanRecord are represented in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
